## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and gunplay balance:

### 🌡️ Dynamic Heat Accumulation & Cooling System
* **Real-time Heat Calculation:** Every shot fired adds a configurable amount of heat to the weapon.
* **Per-Weapon Tuning:** Customize heat generation per individual weapon (Pistols, SMGs, Assault Rifles, Machine Guns, Miniguns, or custom weapons) or use the global default.
* **Natural Cooldown:** Configurable cooldown delay timer before the weapon begins smoothly cooling down at a customizable rate.

### 💨 Multi-Stage Visual PTFX (Smoke & Fire)
* **Stage 1 - Muzzle Smoke:** Exceeding the smoke threshold emits realistic unattached world-space smoke puffs from the weapon barrel/suppressor.
* **Stage 2 - Weapon Flames:** Exceeding the fire threshold ignites persistent looped flames directly attached to the gun frame.
* **Dynamic Bone Resolution:** Automatically detects weapon bone positions with fallback support for addon/custom weapons.

### ⚠️ Overheat Jamming & Reaction Mechanics
* **Overheat Delay & Burn Period:** Weapon stays burning briefly, allowing panic firing before the full overheat reaction triggers.
* **Reaction Animation:** Plays a realistic upper-body weapon check/unjam animation that does not lock the player's legs, allowing continuous running or repositioning.
* **Action Blocking:** Temporarily disables shooting, aiming, melee attacks, and weapon switching during overheat to prevent exploit abuse.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence & Configuration
* **Comprehensive Blacklist:** Easily blacklist melee weapons, tasers, flashlights, petrol cans, and utility gear.
* **Visual 3D Debug Mode:** Built-in 3D bone debug visualization with markers and labels to inspect bone alignment.
* **Fully Standalone:** Zero framework dependencies; natively compatible with ESX, QBCore, Qbox, or Standalone setups.
* **Ultra-Optimized:** Resource runs at 0.00ms idle when unarmed or not shooting.
